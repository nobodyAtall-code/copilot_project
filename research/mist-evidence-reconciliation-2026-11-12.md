## Filesystem/boot notes

- `vendor_a/etc/fstab.default` supports stock EROFS and ext4 for logical partitions. `/system`, `/product` and `/system_ext` point to `vbmeta_system`; `/vendor`, `/odm` and dlkm partitions to `vbmeta`. Stock `/data` is F2FS with wrapped-key file and metadata encryption.
- Discard HyperOS `/product` and `/system` overlay mounts as general LineageOS defaults; do not copy the `mi_ext` grafting strategy without a proven dependency.
- The `/mnt/vendor/persist` mount is **not** permission to distribute its contents. Never stage persist/modemst, keys or calibration.
- Seven boot-chain images were hash-verified, but not fully unpacked or validated for ramdisk fragments, DTBs/DTBO, module load order and AVB geometry. Stock `build.config.msm.mist` contains `SUPER_IMAGE_SIZE=0x10000000`, which **must not** be treated as authoritative dynamic partition group capacity.
